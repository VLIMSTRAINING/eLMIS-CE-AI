01.01
What is eLMIS? System Overview and Background
Overview OpenLMIS Zambia

What is eLMIS?

The Electronic Logistics Management Information System (eLMIS), also known as OpenLMIS, is an open-source platform developed through global collaboration among experts in logistics, public health, eHealth systems, and software development for low-resource settings.

Purpose

eLMIS provides critical logistics information for managers of distribution systems. It monitors the flow of products from the central warehouse through intermediate warehouses to health service delivery points (SDPs) that distribute products to end users.

Zambia Context

The Ministry of Health (MOH) and Zambia Medicines and Medical Supplies Agency (ZAMMSA) implemented eLMIS to streamline operations and improve transparency. The system enables end-to-end visibility of health commodities from origin to delivery. It interoperates with SmartCare Pro and the warehouse management platform (WMS).

Four Integrated Components

  • Central Edition (CE)
  • The Mediator
  • Facility Edition (FE)
  • Mobile application

01.02
What eLMIS Can Do Benefits and Six Rights of Logistics
Benefits Logistics Principles

The Six Rights of Logistics

The basic objective of any logistics system is to provide:

  • The right quantities
  • Of the right commodities
  • In the right condition
  • In the right place
  • At the right time
  • For the right cost

Key Benefits of eLMIS

  • Reduced stock outs and reduced wastage
  • Timely and accurate submission of reports and orders
  • Increased availability and accessibility of pharmaceuticals and medical supplies
  • Improved stock monitoring at all levels of the supply chain
  • Access to accurate, timely, and routine consumption data
  • Real-time logistics management from point of origin to point of consumption
  • Demand forecasting, capacity planning, and modeling based on consumption

01.05
eLMIS R&R Data Flow How Information Moves Through the System
R&R Flow Data Flow ZAMMSA

The R&R Flow (Step by Step)

  1. Store In Charge at health facility creates, authorizes, and submits R&Rs in eLMIS
  2. District Pharmacist reviews and approves R&Rs at the district level
  3. ZAMMSA/LMU approves and converts R&Rs into orders in eLMIS and WMS
  4. Consignment is delivered to the district from ZAMMSA warehouse
  5. Consignment is delivered to the facility from the district

Key Actors in the Flow

  • Health Facilities create and submit R&Rs
  • District (DHO) approve R&Rs at district level
  • ZAMMSA LMU final approval and conversion to order
  • Data Specialists / WMS process order in warehouse system

02.01
Password Security and Account Protection
All Users Security Login

eLMIS is fully auditable. All data is entered and logged by username. In the event of a dispute or loss of a commodity, the system provides an accurate record of all staff who participated in the transaction.

Never share your username or password. If you suspect someone has accessed your account, contact the Help Desk immediately.

Logging Into eLMIS

Access eLMIS at: https://zm-elmis.org/public/pages/login.html

Enter your Username and Password, then click Sign in.

Auto Logout

eLMIS will automatically log you out after 5 minutes of inactivity to prevent unauthorized use.

02.04
Data Entry Rules Numeric Fields, Reasons, Comments, and Errors
Facility Users Data Entry R&R

Numeric Fields

Quantity fields accept numbers only. Typing non-numeric characters will show the message: "Numbers Only".

Reason Fields

Some fields require a reason before you can submit. Reasons are visible to approvers. Enter the reason before clicking Submit or you will not be able to proceed.

Comments

You can add comments to any R&R or report by clicking "Comments" at the bottom right of the screen. A pop-up box will open enter your comment and click Add.

Pop-up Screens (Losses & Adjustments)

For losses and adjustments, click the pen symbol () to open the pop-up. Select the adjustment type, enter the quantity, and click Add. Click Done to save or Cancel to undo.

Error Messages

eLMIS provides instant error feedback. You cannot submit data with incomplete mandatory fields. Errors are flagged on the relevant tab you must go to each marked page and fix all errors before proceeding.

Auto-Calculation

Fields set to "calculated" by the System Administrator are auto-populated (e.g., Stock on Hand is auto-calculated as you enter Beginning Balance, Received, Dispensed, and Losses). These fields cannot be manually edited.

02.05
Page Navigation, Scroll Bar, Export, Print, and Logout
All Users Interface Export

Go To Page

For long R&Rs with multiple pages, page numbers appear at the bottom right of the screen. Click a number to jump to that page, or use the arrow buttons for first/last page. A "go-to-page" function lists long reports in multiples of 25s.

Scroll Bar

A gray horizontal scrollbar at the bottom of each screen allows left-right scrolling. It remains fixed as you scroll up and down, so you can always scroll horizontally without returning to the top.

Export to PDF / Excel

Many reports can be exported. Look for the PDF and Excel buttons at the top right corner of the report screen.

Print

Click the Print button at the top of any screen to print that screen or document.

Logging Out

Always click Logout when finished. eLMIS also auto-logs you out after 5 minutes of inactivity. Do not leave your session open on a shared computer.

03.01
Logging In and Starting an R&R Initiating a Requisition
Facility User Create R&R Requisition

How to Start a New Requisition

  1. Go to https://zm-elmis.org/public/pages/login.html and sign in
  2. Click Requisitions in the top menu
  3. Select Create / Authorize from the dropdown
  4. The "Initiate Report and Requisition" screen opens
  5. "My facility" is selected by default. To create for a supervised facility, click the "My supervised facilities" radio button
  6. Select a Program from the dropdown (e.g., Antiretroviral Drugs, Essential Medicine, HIV Test Kits, Laboratory Commodities)
  7. A list of reporting periods and their R&R status appears
  8. Click Proceed for the period you need to submit
ℹ Facility name is automatically populated when "My facility" is selected and cannot be changed. If "My supervised facilities" is selected, choose from the facility dropdown.

03.02
Filling In a Full Supply R&R Data Entry Fields
Facility User Data Entry Full Supply

Select the Correct Tab

After clicking Proceed, select the Full Supply Products tab (or Non-Full Supply / Regimen tabs as applicable).

Fields to Enter for Each Line Item

  • Beginning Balance: For the first R&R, enter from the paper form. After that, the system auto-populates the previous month's Stock on Hand.
  • Total Quantity Received: Enter from the paper form.
  • Total Quantity Dispensed: Enter from the paper form.
  • Losses and Adjustments: Click the gray pen () icon to open the Losses & Adjustments pop-up. Select type, enter quantity, click Add, then Done.
  • Stock on Hand: Auto-calculated (if system is set to calculated). Otherwise enter from paper form.
  • Requested Quantity: Enter the quantity being requested.
  • Requested Quantity Explanation: Required explanation field.
  • Remarks: Any additional notes.

System-Calculated Fields (read-only)

  • Average Monthly Consumption (AMC)
  • Maximum Stock Quantity
  • Calculated Order Quantity
  • Packs to Ship, Price Per Pack, Total Cost
Navigate between pages using the page number buttons at the bottom of the screen. Click Save at any time to save your progress before submitting.

03.03
Entering Losses and Adjustments
Facility User Data Entry Adjustments

How to Record a Loss or Adjustment

  1. In the R&R, find the product row requiring a loss or adjustment
  2. Click the gray pen icon () in the Losses/Adjustments column
  3. The "Losses and Adjustments" pop-up opens
  4. From the Select Type dropdown, choose the adjustment type
  5. Enter the Quantity
  6. Click Add
  7. The adjustment appears with a running total
  8. Click Done to save, or Cancel to discard

Available Adjustment Types

  • Damaged
  • Transfer In
  • Transfer Out
  • Expired
  • Passed Open-Vial Time Limit
  • Cold Chain Failure
  • Lost
  • Computer Generated (+) or (−)
  • Stolen
  • Clinic Return

04.03
Converting a Requisition to an Order
LMU / ZAMMSA Convert to Order Order Conversion

How to Convert an Approved Requisition to an Order

  1. Click Requisitions → Convert to Order
  2. The "Convert Requisitions to Order" screen opens
  3. Check the checkbox next to the requisition(s) to convert
  4. Click the Convert To Order button
  5. A "Confirm Action" pop-up appears
  6. Click YES to export the order as a CSV file and release it as an order
  7. Click NO to complete the conversion but not release the order for processing
  8. A success message confirms: "The requisition(s) have been successfully converted to Orders"
Clicking NO at step 6 means the requisition is converted but the order will not be processed. Choose YES to fully release the order to the WMS.
